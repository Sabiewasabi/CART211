Why this exists

Free services often make money by collecting data about you and using it to predict what you will do.

The writer Shoshana Zuboff calls this surveillance capitalism. She argues that the extra data these companies collect is turned into predictions about us and sold to advertisers.

This site responds by letting you play along with fake information. Next, you will invent a person for the trackers to follow.

In real life: Your searches, clicks and interests can be stored and linked together.
